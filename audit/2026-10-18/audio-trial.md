# Recorded audio audition — Carriage / Coastal

Preview-only opt-in audition using the two publicly available CC0 HQ MP3 recordings documented in audio-shortlist.md. This is not final adoption or a claim to have auditioned the source WAVs. Carriage uses Yoyodaman234 / 341208; Coastal uses kkenny101 / 852826. The Pacific-coast 587164 preview was excluded because its uploader warns of preview distortion. No synthesized replacement sounds were created.

Edits: cyclic equal-power end/head overlap (2s train, 1.5s waves), RMS target −27dBFS with peak ceiling −12dBFS, MP3 160kbps. The original train clip is stereo, wave loop mono. No claim that absent speech/birds or subjective seamlessness has been verified by listening. Final source choice, original-WAV audition and real iPhone/headphone/speaker listening remain review items. The author should be asked for Freesound sign-in only when original downloads are actually being acquired; a Mac sign-in does not authenticate this cloud process.

No AudioContext or sound fetch until the explicit menu action. Menu contains enable/mute, volume and source credits on its existing glass surface. Page starts muted. Pause/restart synchronize with scene time, night reduces wave level and high frequencies; Carriage uses a small level/filter change from its existing station weights, without inventing a station announcement or outside pass-by effect. One decoded loop and one lowpass/gain chain; no new 3D pass.

Pagehide/visibility suspend sound, pageshow resumes only if previously enabled, and BroadcastChannel claim mutes another audition window. Loading failures leave the visual work available with a retryable audio status. Sources remain on the same origin; credit links lead to the individual Freesound pages. Japanese/English labels follow existing h53lang.

Chromium/SwiftShader interaction verification: initial mute/no fetch, actual enable/mute controls, decode, pause/resume, synthetic pagehide/pageshow and another-tab BroadcastChannel claim passed for both works. These tests do not measure subjective sound quality or Safari's audio-device behavior. Root production pages retain no audition module.
