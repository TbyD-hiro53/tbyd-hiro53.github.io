# Audio review v3 — author feedback on S03 / S04 / S06

Author feedback: S03 needs sand striking nearby as well as wind, with a DUNE-like reference. S04 sounded like human voices. S06 is acceptable if unrelated noises can be removed. The other seven sources received positive feedback; this does not approve every proposed artwork implementation.

S04 file hashes match the original source ledger. The old excerpt was the first 20 seconds. Silero VAD detected speech candidates at 6.822–14.202 seconds. Later wind is very low-level (0:50–1:20: approximately −63 dBFS full-band RMS before filtering). The new 30-second excerpt uses that later segment, a 45 Hz high-pass and +42 dB gain. Speech detection is an aid, not a guarantee; no subjective listening approval of this new sample is claimed. Whisper transcription model acquisition failed with HTTP 403, so no transcription claims are made.

S03 uses the existing wind 376534 plus real sand blown against contact microphones, klankbeeld 244967 (CC BY 4.0). Sources are linked and modifications credited in the review page. Sand starts at 0:40. Wind is low-passed, sand retains the granular high frequencies; no generated noise or film audio is used. An additional genuine dust-storm comparison, samwd 627055 (CC0), was acquired and kept for research. Vrymaa 825860 was researched but its preview download returned 403; it is not used.

S06 uses 4:45–5:15 selected by low short-window RMS variation, with gentle high/low-pass and compression. A same-interval pre-processing comparison is included. RMS levels are matched to approximately −26.8 dBFS. This is a suppression trial, not a claim of complete sound separation. The room tone is not denoised into silence.
