# Audio audition v2 — wind and one-tap control

Author feedback: Carriage's sound direction is accepted; Coastal should add wind. Every sound-equipped work should expose a speaker icon beside its menu, with a slash only when muted and one-tap toggling. Volume remains inside the menu. This is now the common audio-control rule for future sound-equipped works; works without an audio design do not get a nonfunctional speaker button.

Both current previews follow the shared chrome's top-right menu location. The icon has a 44×44 target, 22px drawing, accessible action label and aria-pressed. It uses each work's existing liquid-glass renderer. Hiding controls hides the speaker too; opening an explanation follows the shared reading guard. The outside-dismiss helper allows the sound button to act without consuming its first tap while the menu is open. Other outside taps preserve their previous dismissal behavior.

Carriage keeps the author's auditioned v1 audio asset unchanged. Coastal combines the existing wave recording with Soft Wind by florianreichelt, https://freesound.org/people/florianreichelt/sounds/459977/ . Its individual page states CC0; the public HQ MP3 was obtained without authentication. Source SHA256 e1cf29f5305a79edb5e57fb658c35e307078c392fb6bb6e8e8941e1ea7f67423. Wind is high-passed at 80Hz and placed 4 dB below the wave's actual RMS. Independent cyclic layers are mixed into an 80-second stereo loop with a 3-second end/head overlap, −12 dBFS peak ceiling and 192kbps MP3. This requires one audio buffer/source, not an additional runtime sound simulation. Source credits for both recordings remain available in the menu.

Initial mute, explicit gesture to create AudioContext/download audio, pause/visibility synchronization, cross-tab exclusivity and retry remain. Wind recording technique is not independently established by the uploader description; it is a sourced sound recording, not a newly synthesized noise layer. The author's hearing review of v2 and original-file acquisition/final audio adoption remain separate from browser functional verification.
