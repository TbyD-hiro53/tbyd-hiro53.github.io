# Confluence v11 — fixed-eye Cycles / real-time water experiment

Preview only. The author's white/blue direction and the landscape elements from 『白』 are retained. Three fixed stations use baked static landscape radiance and radial depth, with a separate reflected-eye panorama. Water, touched waves, the rotating discs and their moving direct-light attenuation remain real-time. Dragging changes gaze at the baked eye; moving freely away from it is not supported by the work's navigation.

Before baking, near root components were joined and capped, with local voxel fusion at the thick junctions. A welded five-tier path shoulder replaces disconnected shoulder strips; its outer edge is buried into the ground. The preserved original meshes remain available for picking and initial shadow-map preparation. Forest foliage is still modeled foliage, not photographic footage; this preview does not claim to remove every stylized CG feature.

Each station has a 4096×2048 panorama, a 4096² perspective for its initial direction and a 2048×1024 mirror panorama. Cycles 20 samples, guided OIDN, linear HDR RGBM16 and logarithmic 16-bit radial depth are losslessly transported as gzip-compressed raw RGBA bytes. Diffuse/specular direct light fraction in the depth map's blue channel allows the moving disc shadow to attenuate only direct illumination. Analytic sky is evaluated at infinite-depth rays to avoid quantized sky gradients. All static source geometry below the water plane is clipped out of the mirror bake.

The shader reconstructs the surface depth for the current camera. The live water plane and discs are depth-tested against it. Perspective Z was verified to be axial with an independent constant-distance plane; package conversion corrects this before projection. Seven panorama rays were compared against the static original geometry; they match within normal depth quantization except the intentionally remodeled shoulder.

Only one station's six textures are retained. Their theoretical GPU image budget including color mipmaps is about 243 MiB, in addition to render targets/live scene resources. Uploaded CPU typed arrays are released. Context restoration reloads the page. Unsupported gzip streaming, fragment depth or maximum texture size falls back to the established real-time scene. Auto image resolution may use up to 6M pixels / DPR 2 for baked mode, with the former smaller budget for live fallback. This is a transfer and GPU-memory tradeoff, not a claim of zero load or measured iPhone frame rate.

Source scripts, packed Blender static scene, all runtime dependencies and independent extraction verification belong in the preservation package. Large export JSON is an intermediate and is regenerated from the preceding live engine. Never promote this unapproved experiment to the root production artwork.
