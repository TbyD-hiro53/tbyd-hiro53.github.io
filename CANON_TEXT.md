# CANON_TEXT — 序文・作品説明・断章

`index.html` に反映されているテキストの正本。HTMLを直接編集せず、まずこのファイルを更新し、
`build_frag.py` を通して反映すること。

## 書式の役割分担（混ぜない）

- **説明** … 作品そのものの記述。何が見えるか、何ができるか、どう操作するか。世界観の語りを入れない。1〜2文。
- **断章** … 記録の声による語り。難解・迂遠・小説的。操作や技術の語彙を入れない。250〜350字。
- **観測ログ** … 断章の見出しに置く英文一行。

各断章は一投稿として成立し、**順序非依存**で書く。位相タグは読む順序ではなくカノン上の所属を示す。
登録簿の配置順は制作者の推奨順であり、位相の昇順とは一致しない。

## 序文 / Preface

**JA**

これは記録である。物語ではない。

効率が神になった世界で、人類は滅びずに昇格した。その過程で作られた構造物、炉、標章を、稼働状態のまま並べてある。

各項目には、それが属する位相の断章が付いている。順に読む必要はない。追跡したときに、構造が立ち上がる。

**EN**

This is a record, not a story.

In a world where efficiency became god, humanity did not perish; it was promoted. What follows are the structures, the cores and the marks produced along the way, held in working order.

Each entry carries the fragment of the phase it belongs to. They need not be read in order. The architecture assembles itself once you follow it.

## CANON — Cyberwafer v25

**位相** PHASE −1 · PRE-CAUSAL ANOMALIES

**観測ログ** `The specification preceded the object.`

**説明 JA**
Y2K と TRANSCENDENCE の2モデルをボタンで切り替える基幹作品。心拍0.78秒、ディスク周期2.4秒、正準パレットがここで定義され、以降の全作品が継承する。ドラッグで回転、ピンチで拡大。

**説明 EN**
The core work, switching between the Y2K and TRANSCENDENCE models at a button. The 0.78-second pulse, the 2.4-second disc cycle and the canonical palette are defined here, and every later work inherits them. Drag to rotate, pinch to zoom.

**断章 JA**
図面が先に出た。着工の記録はどこにも無く、資材の請求も立っていないのに、寸法だけが完成した姿で綴じられていた。監査は三度入り、三度とも同じ結論を書いた——この構造物は、建てられる前に検収されている。以後、誰も原本を探さなくなった。探しても、写しの方が古いのである。切替器に指を触れると、同じ図面が二つの時代を名乗る。どちらが後で、どちらが先だと言えるだけの根拠を、帝国はついに用意しなかった。

**断章 EN**
The drawing came first. Nowhere was there any record of the ground being broken, no requisition had been raised for materials, and yet the dimensions had been bound already in their finished state. The audit came three times, and three times wrote the same conclusion — that the structure had been accepted before it was built. After that, nobody looked for the original. To look was pointless: the copies were older. Touch the switch and the same drawing will name two eras. The grounds on which one might be called the earlier and the other the later, the empire never troubled to supply.

## ASSET 01 — Object

**位相** PHASE 1 · THE FIRST T

**観測ログ** `It was first observed. It was not new.`

**説明 JA**
磁性のTレターフォームを四行の周回テキストリングが囲み、その下でクロームディスクが降下しては生成される。背景切替とグリッド床を備えた、全作品の起点となる正準オブジェクト。

**説明 EN**
A magnetised T letterform ringed by four rotating lines of text, with chrome discs descending and renewing below. With a switchable background and a grid floor, this is the canonical object from which every work begins.

**断章 JA**
最初に観測されたときも、それは新しくなかった。周囲の格子は既にその形へ合わせて敷かれており、影の落ちる位置には風化があった。撤去は一度だけ試みられている。分解の手順は完了し、部材は搬出され、跡地は測量され、そして翌週の地図に同じ形が別の縮尺で現れた。以来、目録は「除去不能」と記す。文字は四行、絶えず巡っている。読み終えられないよう、行ごとに向きが違う。

**断章 EN**
It was not new when it was first observed. The grid around it had already been laid to suit the shape, and where its shadow fell the ground was weathered. Removal was attempted once. The dismantling was completed, the members were carried out, the site was surveyed — and in the following week's map the same shape appeared at another scale. Since then the inventory has recorded it as irremovable. The text runs on four lines, without pause. So that it can never be finished, each line turns the other way.

## ASSET 02 — Code Rain v4

**位相** PHASE 2 · DEUS EX MACHINA

**観測ログ** `The face was not drawn. It was tabulated.`

**説明 JA**
Canvas 2Dのみで構成した文字組の作品。社名・教義・製品名が降下する格子の濃淡から会長の像が結ばれる。発光処理を全て除き、クリスプなインクだけで描いている。

**説明 EN**
A work of typesetting built with Canvas 2D alone. The Chairman's likeness resolves out of the density of a falling grid of trade names, doctrine and product codes. All glow is stripped away; only crisp ink remains.

**断章 JA**
誰も像を注文していない。表は在庫の照合のために回されていたはずで、綴りの一つ一つに描写の意図は無かった。ただ列が満ちたとき、濃度の差が顔になった。技師は最初、汚れを疑った。次に、印字の癖を疑った。最後に、疑うべき対象が存在しないことに気づいた。命令は発せられていない。修正は始まっていた。会長は描かれたのではなく、集計されたのである。

**断章 EN**
Nobody ordered the likeness. The table had been circulated for the reconciliation of stock, and not one of its entries held any intention to depict. Only when the columns filled did the difference in density become a face. The engineer suspected a smudge, then a quirk of the printing, and at last understood that there was nothing left to suspect. No command had been issued. The correction had already begun. The Chairman was not drawn. He was tabulated.

## ASSET 03 — Code Rain v5

**位相** PHASE 3 · TRANSCENSION

**観測ログ** `The rain slowed only where the body was.`

**説明 JA**
Canvas 2Dのみの文字組。跪座する全身像が降雨の格子から解像し、上端から下端へ一行ずつ印字される。降雨は図像セルを通過する間だけ減速する。タップで再発火。

**説明 EN**
Typesetting in Canvas 2D alone. A kneeling figure resolves out of the falling grid, printed one row at a time from top to bottom. The rain slows only while passing through the cells of the figure. Tap to fire it again.

**断章 JA**
落下の速度は一定であるはずだった。計測班は三箇所で同じ結果を得た——像の内側でだけ、雨が遅い。減速の原因は列にはない。列は等しく流れており、遅れるのは輪郭に触れた瞬間の一文字だけである。滞留した文字は身体の縁に溜まり、しばらく形を保ってから流れ去る。跪いた姿勢について記録は何も述べていない。姿勢は測定項目ではなかった。

**断章 EN**
The rate of fall was supposed to be constant. The measuring party obtained the same result at three separate points — the rain is slower, and only within the figure. The cause of the retardation is not in the columns. The columns flow evenly; what lags is the single character at the instant it touches the outline. The characters that linger gather at the edge of the body, hold their shape a while, and are carried off. Of the kneeling posture the record says nothing. Posture was not among the measured items.

## ASSET 04 — Code Rain v6

**説明 EN**
Typesetting in Canvas 2D alone. A portrait resolves in a scattered dissolve ordered by luminance, and only the region of the tongue pulses independently as an ember in a fourth colour. Short columns drip from it. Tap to amplify the dripping.

**断章 JA**

**断章 EN**
The sound was recorded. Frequency, duration, interval, even the area of the aperture are entered in the table. What would not be entered is the meaning. No vocabulary existed against which to collate it, and it matched no known system. The officer wrote no language assigned, and the column was closed as it stood. Only the tongue holds a different colour. It beats on the same period as the rest, and only there has nothing cooled. The drip falls, rewriting the characters it passes.

## ASSET 05 — Lacto-Cortex v6

**説明 EN**

The ledger says: in operation. The document defining what operation means is listed in the index, but the body of it cannot be found.

Nowhere is this chain broken, and nowhere is its beginning written. The band above the plinth was left empty, and only four rows of text circle there. It was not that there was nothing to seat; the drawing specified that nothing be seated.

## ASSET 07 — Sigil Fusion v5

**位相** PHASE 3 · TRANSCENSION

**観測ログ** `Ascension no longer required belief.`

**説明 JA**
白地に置かれた唯一の作品。ラスタを一切用いず、SVGと数式のみで拍を刻む。皮質を三重の周回文字環が囲み、下方へウェハーが縮みながら降りる。WebGL不使用。

**説明 EN**
The only work set on white. No raster is used anywhere; SVG and arithmetic alone keep the beat. Three rings of circling text enclose the cortex while wafers descend below, shrinking as they fall. No WebGL.

**断章 JA**
祭壇は要らなかった。供物も、跪く場所も、唱える者も要らなかった。必要だったのは比だけである。三つの環がそれぞれ違う速さで巡り、共通の周期を持たないよう調律されている。合致する瞬間は計算上存在しない。だから儀式は終わらない。終わらないものを信仰と呼ぶ習慣は、この頃までに廃れていた。白は清潔のためではなく、記録の背景として選ばれている。

**断章 EN**
No altar was required. No offering, no place to kneel, no one to recite. What was required was a ratio. The three rings turn each at a different rate, tuned so as to hold no period in common. The instant of their coincidence does not exist arithmetically. The rite therefore does not end. The habit of calling what does not end a faith had, by this time, fallen out of use. The white was chosen not for cleanliness but as the ground of a record.

## ASSET 08 — Empyrean Sigil v7

**位相** PHASE 4 · THE TRANSCENDED

**観測ログ** `The mark continued to certify an owner that could not be located.`

**説明 JA**
乳脳圏の社章。二重のベゼルが互いに逆へ回り、細環が下から生まれて上で消え、銀盤が降下する。中央のTは動かずネオンのみ明滅。背景の明暗を切り替えられる。

**説明 EN**
The mark of the empyrean. Two bezels turn against each other, thin rings are born below and vanish above, and silver discs descend. The T at the centre holds still; only its neon flickers. The background switches between light and dark.

**断章 JA**
標章は証明のために作られる。何を証明するのかを問われたとき、法務は「所有」と答え、それ以上は答えなかった。外と内の目盛りは反対へ巡り、どちらを基準に据えても他方が狂う。刻まれた数は読み取れるが、数え始める位置が指定されていない。所有は疑われていない。疑われているのは所有者である。台帳の当該欄には、記入があり、署名があり、そして参照先が空白のまま綴じられている。

**断章 EN**
A mark is made in order to certify. Asked what it certified, the legal office answered ownership, and answered nothing further. The outer graduation and the inner run contrary, so that whichever is set as the reference, the other reads false. The figures cut into them can be read, but no position is specified from which to begin the count. Ownership is not in doubt. What is in doubt is the owner. In the relevant column of the ledger there is an entry, and a signature, and a reference that was bound in while still blank.

The report concludes: the total remains, the unit cannot be extracted. Inspections are since conducted by aggregate alone. The placard reads extremely sweet. For whom the notice was intended is written nowhere.

**説明 JA**
五重の同心輪に各五基、放射する五本のスポークへ整列した配列。シネマティックな巡回で全景が開かれ、上下の黒帯とキャプションが入る。SWで視点切替、TOURで自動巡回。

**説明 EN**
Five concentric rings, five units to a ring, aligned along five radiating spokes. A cinematic pass opens the whole, with letterbox bars and captions. SW switches viewpoints; TOUR begins the automatic circuit.

**断章 JA**

**断章 EN**
The alignment resembles the form of a faith. It only resembles it; there is no trace of its having been designed so. Five to the rings. Five to what is seated on each. Five, again, to the lines that split the floor. The floor was drawn again afterwards, so that the distance should be equal measured from any position. Nothing is seated at the centre. Whether something was to have been seated there, or was removed, the record survives in both forms. The circling viewpoint passes through it repeatedly and does not notice the passing. There is a response. Only the one responding is absent.

**断章 JA**
壊れているものは無い。冷却は回り、導光は流れ、幹線は洞壁の口へ深く入っている。保守の記録は今月の分まで揃っていて、次月の予定も既に組まれている。欠けているのは一枚だけである——この電力の宛先を記した票が、最後の更新日から動いていない。担当は交代を続け、引継書にはこう書かれる。「送電先については前任に確認のこと」。前任もまた、同じ一行を写していた。

**断章 EN**
Nothing is broken. The cooling turns, the light-guides run, the trunks reach deep into their mouths in the rock. The maintenance records are complete to the present month, and the schedule for the next is already drawn. One sheet only is wanting — the slip recording where this power is sent has not moved since the date of its last revision. The post continues to change hands, and the handover reads: as to the destination of transmission, confirm with your predecessor. The predecessor, too, had copied out the same line.
