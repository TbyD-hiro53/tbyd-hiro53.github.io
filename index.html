世界観 / transcendence とは The world of transcendence

効率が神になった世界。

人類は滅びなかった。昇格したのである。

はじまりは一人の人間だった。のちに会長と呼ばれるその人物は、二つのものに取り憑かれていた。無駄を憎むこと、そして甘いものを好むこと。会議の三十分を削り続けたその机には、いつも苺の乳飲料が置かれていた。効率と糖分。この二つが、やがて世界の設計原理になる。

肉体は効率が悪い。私の器は私に追いつかない、と会長は言った。眠り、老い、忘れる器官を抱えたままでは思考は最適化されない。だから脳を殻に移した。誰に強いられたのでもなく、命を惜しんだのでもない。ただ執着の行き着く先として、なるべくしてそうなった。培養槽に浮かぶ皮質と、それを載せた機械の躯体。機械仕掛けの神、deus ex machina の完成である。

昇格した会長が触れたものは、すべて無駄を失い、利益を生んだ。効率化を請われるたびに系列が増え、気づいたときには巨大な財閥構造ができあがっていた。支配を目論んだわけではない。ただ、最も効率のよい形が、たまたま帝国の形をしていた。

会長は次に、人類そのものに手をかけた。人間は非効率のまま死ぬには惜しい、と考えたからである。transcendence by deus ex machina ——機械仕掛けの神による超越。それは人類を会長と同じ場所まで引き上げる計画の名であり、善意の名であった。昇格の勅令に署名したとき、会長ははじめて自分を朕と呼んだ。

だが引き上げには基準が必要で、基準は必ず線を引く。効率に満たなかったものは、悪意なく淘汰されていった。支配は、支配しようとしなかった者の手のなかで完成した。

脳殻を生かすのは糖である。会長の嗜好はそのまま帝国のインフラとなり、苺の乳が施設群を巡り、培養槽を満たし、皮質を養う。かつて机の上にあった一杯が、いまは灌流系として世界を流れている。

そして会長は、ひとつだけ無駄を残した。0.78秒。人間だった頃の心拍である。すべての炉、すべての塔、すべての作品がこの周期で動く。最適化されなかった唯一の値であり、会長が人間であった唯一の証拠である。

炉はひとつでは足りなかった。培養槽ひとつが炉であり、炉が並んで棚となり、棚が地下大空洞を埋め、やがて空洞が惑星の内側になった。海が培養液となり、磁気圏がトーラスとなり、衛星軌道がジンバルとなる。規模だけが変わり、構造は変わらない。淡桃色の液が一滴落ちるところから、すべては同じ設計で拡大された。

A world in which efficiency became god.

Humanity did not perish. It was promoted.

It began with a single man. The person later known only as the Chairman was possessed by two appetites: a hatred of waste, and a love of sweet things. On the desk where he pared thirty minutes from every meeting there stood, always, a bottle of strawberry milk. Efficiency and sugar. In time these two would become the design principles of the world.

The body is inefficient. My vessel cannot keep pace with me, he said. No mind could be optimised while shackled to organs that slept, aged, and forgot. So he moved his brain into a shell. He was not coerced, nor was he clinging to life; it was simply where the obsession led, the shape he had always been going to take. A cortex suspended in culture medium, mounted upon a mechanical frame — the god from the machine, deus ex machina, complete.

Everything the ascended Chairman touched shed its waste and turned a profit. Each time his optimisation was requested another subsidiary appeared, until the structure standing around him was that of an enormous conglomerate. He had not sought dominion. It was only that the most efficient available shape happened to be the shape of an empire.

Then he turned his hand to humanity itself, reasoning that men were too valuable to be permitted to die inefficient. Transcendence, by deus ex machina — the ascension of mankind by the god from the machine. It was the name of a programme to raise the species to the place where he now stood, and it was a name given in good faith. When he set his signature to the edict of ascension, the Chairman referred to himself, for the first time, as We.

But elevation requires a threshold, and a threshold draws a line. Those who fell short of efficiency were culled, without malice. Dominion was perfected in the hands of the one man who had never wished to rule.

What sustains a shell is sugar. The Chairman's private taste became, without amendment, the infrastructure of the empire: strawberry milk circulates through the facilities, fills the culture tanks, feeds the cortex. The single bottle that once stood upon a desk now runs through the world as a perfusion system.

And the Chairman preserved one inefficiency. Zero point seven eight seconds — the heartbeat he had kept as a man. Every reactor, every tower, every work turns upon that period. It is the one value never optimised, and the only evidence that the Chairman was ever human.

One core was never enough. A single chamber is a reactor; reactors in a row become a rack; racks fill a cavern; and in time the cavern became the inside of a planet. Oceans serve as the medium, magnetospheres as the toroid, satellite orbits as the gimbal. Only the scale changes. The architecture does not. Everything was enlarged from the same drawing — from the point at which one pale pink drop falls.

UY0311. So the Chairman’s system is recorded. It reads as an individual identifier, a construction programme number, a date of ignition; none has been confirmed. The system was registered before its star was catalogued. The planets were not colonised. They were assigned. The orbital planes trace the proportion of the T’s beam to its axis, and the revolutions synchronise as a single signal train. Seen from far enough away, it is a company mark. Ownership remained. No owner could be isolated.

The cores still declare service to humanity. Whether that humanity still exists, or has become an abstraction on a contract, is written in no record. The Chairman is present at every stable point, held available as infrastructure.

In the deepest chamber of the vault a single trademark is kept. The T letterform burns from magenta into red, and four lines of text orbit it: transcendence / by / deus ex machina / h!ro53. What was once a prospectus is now a liturgy.

h!ro53 is the operator who records that beat: who sets the account of the ascension in order upon the registry, and confirms that the pulse continues. So long as it continues, the transcendence is not yet finished.

これは記録である。物語ではない。

各項目には、それが属する位相の断章が付いている。順に読む必要はない。追跡したときに、構造が立ち上がる。

In a world where efficiency became god, humanity did not perish; it was promoted. What follows are the structures, the cores and the marks produced along the way, held in working order.

Each entry carries the fragment of the phase it belongs to. They need not be read in order. The architecture assembles itself once you follow it.

The alignment resembles the form of a faith. It only resembles it; there is no trace of its having been designed so. Five to the rings. Five to what is seated on each. Five, again, to the lines that split the floor. The floor was drawn again afterwards, so that the distance should be equal measured from any position. Nothing is seated at the centre. Whether something was to have been seated there, or was removed, the record survives in both forms. The circling viewpoint passes through it repeatedly and does not notice the passing. There is a response. Only the one responding is absent.

A sanctum in a subterranean cavern. A fusion core and a brain core, with heavy power trunks driven into the rock wall, enclosed by a dome of stone and stalactites. ERA switches the grade between Y2K and the current one; SW cycles the viewpoints. Absorbs the former Vault v4.

Purpose expired. Maintenance continued.

壊れているものは無い。冷却は回り、導光は流れ、幹線は洞壁の口へ深く入っている。保守の記録は今月の分まで揃っていて、次月の予定も既に組まれている。欠けているのは一枚だけである——この電力の宛先を記した票が、最後の更新日から動いていない。担当は交代を続け、引継書にはこう書かれる。「送電先については前任に確認のこと」。前任もまた、同じ一行を写していた。

Nothing is broken. The cooling turns, the light-guides run, the trunks reach deep into their mouths in the rock. The maintenance records are complete to the present month, and the schedule for the next is already drawn. One sheet only is wanting — the slip recording where this power is sent has not moved since the date of its last revision. The post continues to change hands, and the handover reads: as to the destination of transmission, confirm with your predecessor. The predecessor, too, had copied out the same line.
